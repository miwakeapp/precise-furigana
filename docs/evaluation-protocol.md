# Independent alignment evaluation

Historical checkpoint: this panel was retired as a holdout during parity-first development on 2026-10-09. Its original artifacts and scores remain unchanged. Reviewed source evidence may now inform development; neither the old labels nor their scores provide an independent accuracy test of later generators. A new superiority claim requires a fresh evaluation.

The target is the finest linguistically supported spelling–reading correspondence. A character boundary is useful when independent lexical, morphological, or phonological evidence supports its sound assignment in this context. Phonetic ateji, name readings, and conventional contractions can justify character-level boundaries. A dictionary's editorial choice to print whole-word ruby does not by itself invalidate those boundaries. Conversely, arbitrary residual assignment in a genuinely indivisible reading is false precision. Whole positional numeral units and defensible place-value digit correspondences are both accepted for evaluation; numeral typography must not create an apparent superiority advantage.

## Frozen family holdout

Before adjudication and subsequent tuning, a deterministic sample was frozen from initial-build SHA-256 `8472c2616b1fd67a8914ca6b1c5653af548d048066485db8ca35347b7b5008ff`, based on source release `3.6.2+20261005200550`. The seed is `precise-furigana-independent-family-audit-2026-10-09-v1`. The selection manifest SHA-256 is `94efc7ba4aa1fdc910571ef54781f0aa1f669470a94173ac12d8651f34422c61`.

The sample contains 427 spelling–reading span families: 300 coarser discrepancy families across four diagnostic strata, 60 incompatible families, seven eligible finer families, and 60 exact-agreement controls. A family here is a minimal discrepant spelling and normalized reading span, or a whole spelling–reading pair for an agreement control. Overlapping selected spans are excluded, but orthographic variants and different readings can still share an underlying lexical family. Entry-level aggregation accompanies span-level results; neither makes all sampled linguistic phenomena independent. Previously documented regression examples and discussed leading families were excluded during selection. During blind reading, two previously exposed orthographic relatives were identified and excluded before scoring, leaving 425 eligible families. The original manifest is unchanged and exclusions are recorded separately. This deliberate stratification targets known difficult regions and is not a simple random sample of the dictionary.

All 570 complete entry IDs containing selected families are withheld from development example inspection. Generator input dictionaries remain available normally; the withheld material is adjudication and example-based feedback, not ordinary unlabeled lexical data. Implementation agents may inspect aggregate corpus change counts and the safe excluded-ID list, but must not read the blind packet, selection manifest, or labels before the implementation is frozen for evaluation.

## Adjudication

The adjudicator initially sees only the target spelling and reading, a contextual source entry, and licensed JMdict/KANJIDIC2 information, including name readings. Neither generator nor reference alignments are shown. Labels record accepted segmentations, linguistic rationale, and uncertainty. Alternate segmentations are allowed when both sound correspondences are defensible; unresolved judgments do not silently become labels favoring either system. Missing or silent particles can create a rendering convention rather than a unique sound boundary; accepted alternatives or uncertainty prevent such conventions from creating spurious quality wins.

The first pass labeled all 427 rows before seeing outputs. A second AI adjudicator independently labeled 140 moderate or uncertain rows without seeing first-pass labels or either system's outputs. Eleven potentially exposed rows were excluded from that reviewer's assignment using a recorded earlier development sample. The second adjudicator had previously helped implement source-derived features; this is independent annotation, not an independent organization or human expert panel. Disagreements were reconciled using the pinned source dictionaries before evaluation. Both original judgment files are preserved alongside the reconciled labels.

The frozen reconciled labels contain 283 high-confidence, 123 moderate-confidence, and 21 unresolved rows before the two exposure exclusions. Their SHA-256 is `8a2bc9c8cfa3bc508273be2d69de1383f5c6a3a6cff4859c07d35b09752a9530`; the first-pass SHA-256 is `3b29a407d35fb00ced1c3675497f53ef14ac290f686a4e90321e3435837d8d8e`, and the independent second-review SHA-256 is `aca93342c4b61f6ba43c26cc23eaed1b3c6220642ac61fdec9fcac12076cc6a4`. Labels and reference-containing materials remain quarantined under `.cache/holdout-v1/`.

After labels are fixed and the generator is frozen, `node scripts/evaluate-holdout.mjs FROZEN-DATASET.json` evaluates both systems against the same labels. It verifies the label checkpoint and original selection dataset, locates each target span using paired written/reading coordinates, and expands literal kana runs into implicit boundaries. It reports missed supported boundaries separately from assertions absent from the accepted label. That latter count means unsupported by the annotation, not a separately proven linguistic error. Accepted alternatives minimize unsupported assertions before missed boundaries, so explicitly accepted typography cannot create a quality advantage. Pareto comparisons preserve tradeoffs instead of silently choosing an arbitrary cost for false versus missing boundaries.

Report high-confidence and broader resolved results separately, with unresolved cases excluded explicitly. Report both span-level and entry-level outcomes. Frequency-weighted or corpus-level conclusions require an additional sampling model; raw family counts are not pair-weighted accuracy estimates. Agreement controls can expose shared errors that discrepancy-only review would miss. Keep implementation-development examples and final held-out evidence separate. Initial evaluation reports aggregates only, preserving individual held-out judgments from implementation feedback.

The panel is adjudicated by AI assistants and has not been independently double-annotated by human Japanese lexicographers. Any quantitative conclusion must state that limitation. Confidence intervals describe sampling uncertainty, not annotation correctness or protection against every related-family dependency. Changes made in response to held-out findings require a fresh holdout before claiming a new independent test result.
